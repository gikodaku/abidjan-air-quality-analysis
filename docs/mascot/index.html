Kos the leopard

The AAQA mascot at each AQI level. Its fur, health, scene and animation follow the air quality and the health advice. Animations stop when the system's "reduce motion" setting is on.

Good

AQI 0–50

Outdoors in the sun, jumping for joy: enjoy the fresh air.

Advice: Air quality is satisfactory. No restrictions on outdoor activities.

kos-good.svg

Moderate

AQI 51–100

Outdoors, calm, a light haze: fine for almost everyone.

Advice: Acceptable. Unusually sensitive people should limit prolonged outdoor exertion.

kos-moderate.svg

Unhealthy for sensitive groups

AQI 101–150

Worried, runny nose, sweating: sensitive people should take it easy.

Advice: Children, older adults, people with asthma and pregnant women: reduce intense outdoor exertion.

kos-usg.svg

Unhealthy

AQI 151–200

Sick and coughing behind an FFP2 mask: wear one if you stay out long.

Advice: Everyone may feel effects. Avoid outdoor sport; wear an FFP2 mask during prolonged exposure.

kos-unhealthy.svg

Very unhealthy

AQI 201–300

At home behind the window, mask, blanket and thermometer: stay indoors.

Advice: Health alert: stay indoors with windows closed. Sensitive groups: no outdoor activity.

kos-very_unhealthy.svg

Hazardous

AQI 301–500

A zombie in dark smog with flies around: health emergency.

Advice: Health emergency: everyone should avoid outdoor exposure.
